# Watching Ishai — guide for Claude

Live AI-written pixel-art reality feed on a free Cloudflare Worker. Pushing to `main` deploys automatically.
Owner (the director): udinigit. Read `docs/SCENE_GUIDELINES.md` before adding or changing any scenery, characters, props or animations.

## Repository
- `source/public/index.html` — the page (all drawing, animation, sound). `source/public/director.html` — director page. `source/public/sandbox.html` — test stage.
- `source/src/worker.template.js` — story engine (AI prompt, validation, director goals, plan merge, real-world data).
- `story/season.md`, `story/plan.json` — season arcs and the day's beats (written by the daily planner).
- `source/public/icons/` — favicons and `og.png` (link preview for WhatsApp etc.), embedded by the build and served by the worker. Favicon = the studio eye; preview = Ishai under the eye.
- `src/vendor/` — third-party code the worker imports.
- `src/index.js` — generated. Build with `cd source && node build.mjs`. Never edit `src/index.js` by hand.
- Bump `VERSION` and commit with a clear message; Cloudflare deploys in ~30 s and open pages auto-reload.

## Non-negotiables
- Reality show of a real life ("The Truman Show"), not a sitcom: realistic people, physics and logistics. Ishai never knows he is filmed.
- No teleportation: every place is on the world map (see the guidelines) with real exits, entries and ways to get there.
- Follow `docs/SCENE_GUIDELINES.md` for anything visual.
- Fictional brands only (Cooola, HummusPrime, Wee Laddie Virgin Scotch, A Butcher Is Born, Tahini Tower Savings Bank, Yalla Yoga, FluffNap, Herzl Hardware, Kfar Kofi, Radio Herzl 88.2, ateBite (food delivery), Everything Else (store), Shuk Shalom (market), NESflicks (streaming), 8bitTV (Channel 8), Boolify (music station), RGBeat (Channel 6, music clips), PicSell (pictures, posters, printed shirts), …). Never real brands or their look-alike trade dress.
- Narration (`text`) is dev-only; viewers see the picture, spoken lines and the hosts (Zohar, Oren).
- Keep the free tiers in mind: Workers AI ~10k neurons/day, KV 1k writes/day.

## How moments are written (AI budget)
Two writers when the `GEMINI_API_KEY` secret is set (Cloudflare dashboard → Workers → ishai → Settings → Variables and Secrets): **Google Gemini (free tier, the newest stable Flash-Lite, or `GEMINI_MODEL`) writes the story** (blocks, live moments) and **Cloudflare Workers AI writes the hosts** (`addHosts()`: one call for a whole block's comments, plus the studio recaps and callers). Each is the other's backup (`runModel(env, messages, maxTokens, role)`); a Gemini 429 pauses it until its quota resets (midnight Pacific). The story prompt then leaves the hosts out (`HOSTS_APART`). Without the key everything runs on Cloudflare in one reply, as before. `/api/health` → `writersSetup` and `usage.gemini`. A Gemini call that fails falls back silently, so `tick()` saves the failures and the pause in the state (`s.geminiErrs`, last 8, and `s.geminiOutUntil`): memory is per isolate and `/api/health` may be a different one. Health shows `writersSetup.geminiErrors` and `usage.gemini.failed` / `failedBy` (per HTTP status).

Workers AI output costs ~6.5x input, so the engine avoids AI calls where it can (`tick()` in the worker, in this order):
- Night (asleep, 22:00-06:00): `nightEvent()` picks a sleep moment from a list. No AI.
- Planned beats: a beat's `preview.moments` (or `script`) are PLAYED AS WRITTEN once the beat starts (`scriptEvent()`): Ishai first walks there one place at a time (`travelEvent()`, no AI), then each moment plays in turn; the live AI then continues the scene and winds it down. The daily planner writes these moments as the finished scene.
- Live moments (one AI call each, spontaneous): radio/TV on (`liveCue()` + `onAirFor()`: a real headline, the real weather or the real station's genre is read by the DJ / Channel 9 and Ishai reacts; otherwise the AI writes the on-air line in `onAir`), and the moment the rain starts or stops. The on-air line is put first in `lines` with `who: "radio"` or `"tv"`; the page plays it (and keeps its own DJ quiet for 4 minutes). Between live radio/TV moments he just listens (`quietMedia()`, no AI).
- Everything else: blocks of up to 5 moments in one AI call (`askBlock()`, `s.queue`), shortened before the next planned beat. Each queued moment is validated when it plays; the block is dropped when anything else happens (a live moment, a beat starts, weather flips, a director note, stuck). A moment that breaks a rule in a new block is skipped and the next one takes over; a block with no usable moment falls back to one moment the old way.
- Places with music: the bar plays a real 24/7 rock station (Radio Paradise Rock Mix; backups from radio-browser in `s.world.barStations`), the café soft rock / pop at low volume (SomaFM Left Coast 70s, Radio Paradise Mellow Mix; backups `cafeStations`), the apartment radio as before. Every stream fades down quickly while anyone talks (`TALK_AT`, set by each typed character of speech) and comes back after; where the browser can't change the volume (iOS) it pauses and resumes instead.
- Rides: `bus` and `taxi` are real places. A story hop like street > market passes through one by itself on the page (`route()`, `rideKind()`), so the AI only uses them when something happens on board (one moment, at most two). `ride` on a public event tells the page which vehicle the text mentions. A friend riding with him follows him to the stop and gets on after him (`comp.boarded` in `compUpdate()`), then sits beside him on board (`rideMate()`). The taxi is shown from outside: a small yellow car, the size of the street's cars, driving through the looping city (`taxiSide()` over `cityDrive()`), with the driver, Ishai and a friend as heads in the windows.
- Planned moments may carry `delivery`, `message` and `update`, like the AI's. `update` ({"memory": "...", "mood": -1|0|1}, on the last moment with that person) is the only way a planned scene changes someone's memory and relationship.
- The story prompt carries the show's longer memory (`storySoFar()`: `s.yesterdaySum`, `s.daySum`, `PLAN.arcs`, clipped); the hosts' call gets a short `s.daySum`.
- What only the narration says: the story prompt tells the writer that anything in `text` the picture can't show (something brought or handed over, wet clothes, a smell, a sound, a message) must be named in `lines`, and the hosts' prompt tells them to name it when nobody does. `unseenHosts()` in `tick()` is the no-AI net: in a moment with talking, if the narration has someone holding, carrying, bringing or handing over something (`UNSEEN_CUE`), or soaked, and no line mentions it, a host adds one ("Dana's got a damp coat and a small terracotta pot..., in case you missed it.").
- Silent moments (no lines, no hosts: travel, waiting, quiet radio/TV, anything the writers left bare) get host lines in `tick()` with no AI (`quietHosts()`, reading out `text`). Viewers can't read `text`, so bare moments must never reach them. Inside a moment, the page fills the rest of the silence: see "Hosts fill the silence".
- A beat with `sceneAt` has a fixed start (the Thursday 17:00 improv class): Ishai travels from `startAt`, then waits with no AI until the story clock reaches `sceneAt`. The story clock runs 5 minutes ahead of the cron tick, and ticks land on the 6-minute marks, so `sceneAt` 16:58 plays at 16:59. The class uses the bar-only action `improv`, which only planned moments can set.
- History course (Strange But True, `story/course.json`: 96 topics in 7 strands that take turns, and the first lecture, written by hand): Sundays, Tuesdays and Wednesdays at 11:00, skipping holidays (`nextLectureAt()`). `courseTick()` runs in `tick()`: up to 30 hours ahead one AI call writes the lecture from the Wikipedia article (`generateLecture()`, about 13,000 to 16,000 tokens). Under 1,400 words it adds a second topic of the same strand; over 7,000 it splits into Part 1 and Part 2. `lectureMoments()` adds the break in the courtyard, the announcement of the next lecture and the walk out, and `scheduleLecture()` turns it into a goal with `course: true` (9 moments, about 48 minutes). The lecture goes ahead of any beat that has not begun. Missed: it moves to the next slot. Lines may come from `student1`-`student8` (nameless, `action: lecture` only). `publicState.course` feeds the blackboard, the courtyard sign and the SUMMARY tab. After a lecture its `note` goes into `s.course.memory` (last 10); `courseNote()` offers one to the AI at most once a day, only with company, each at most twice. The director page has the controls; `/api/lecture` (dev only) gives the sandbox the coming lecture.
- A beat with `sceneAt`: if he is with someone at the start, he excuses himself and goes.
- People: new `SEED_CAST` entries join the running show automatically. `phoneOnly` people (Gadi) are never drawn; a `message` in the AI's reply becomes a first line with `via` (text or email). Regulars are `SEED_CAST` entries with `regular`, matched by `REG` and `MUTTER` in the page.
- Shopping: Shuk Shalom market (food, CAM-10) and the Everything Else store next to it (everything except food, CAM-11) are a bus/taxi ride from the Herzl Street stop (`MAP.street.market` is a ride; the market has its own stop). Opening hours are enforced in `validate()`.
- ateBite food delivery: ordering is a moment on his phone/laptop; when the courier arrives the moment has `"delivery": true` (set by the AI, or detected from the text) and the page shows the orange-helmeted courier ringing, stepping in with the bag and leaving (`courierUpdate()`). Couriers also ride past on the roads now and then. Preview: `?loc=apartment&pose=idle&delivery=12`.
- The gym TV is live like the home TV: when Ishai is on the treadmill (`gym/exercising`) Channel 9 news/weather lines are written live and he reacts.
- AI replies are read by `parseAI()`: strict JSON, then `jsonrepair` (copied into `src/vendor/jsonrepair`; there is no npm step). A cut-off reply is cut at its last `}`, so its unfinished item is lost and no half sentence airs. `validate()` also splits an action like "walking with noa".
- `/api/health` shows `usage` (today's AI calls, tokens, estimated neurons, moments, no-AI moments, wasted block moments, `drops` by reason, `jsonFixed`, `jsonBad`, `blockSaved`), `block`, `lastBlockDrop` and `writers` (who wrote the last 10 moments).

## Everyone sees the same thing (sync)
- The page never reads the device clock for timing: `NOW()` is the server's time (`/api/time`, `syncClock()`). Use `NOW()`, not `Date.now()`, and pass it to `new Date(...)`.
- `schedule()` works out each moment's start and each line's slot from the moments alone (`TL`, `talkPlan()`, `lineMs()`): a line's slot fits its typing and its voice, and a long talk pushes the next moment back. Playback waits for those times (`untilT()`) and never for a device's speech. A viewer who arrives late joins by the clock, mid-line, and gets a voice only from the next line.
- Studio recaps, the auto Studio break and callers start at times worked out from the moments, not from what one browser sees (`studioStart()`, `showStart()`, `callStart()`, `slotAfter()`). The picture-in-picture recap plays on even UTC hours, the full break on odd ones. Don't add `Math.random()` or per-browser checks to anything timed; the 📺 STUDIO button is the one personal exception.
- Movement runs in fixed 33 ms steps on that clock (`advance()`, `STEP`): anything that moves must take its time from `NOW()`, never `performance.now()` or `setTimeout`, because `NOW()` is the simulated time while steps run (`SIM`). A refresh or a returning tab replays the current moment fast and unseen (`joinMoment()`), starting 12 s before it. `update()` may not touch sound or anything a replay would repeat. Companions and the dog move during `render()`, so the replay renders every third step.
- Sound effects are picked from the 25 s clock slot, not at random.
- Voices: `Sound.say()` tries the speaker's own voice and falls back once to the device's default if it fails or hasn't started in 2.5 s. If the browser blocks speech, the button says TAP FOR VOICES. The sandbox status line shows `Sound.voiceInfo()`.
- Can't match exactly: voices finishing, the room-sound loops, real radio streams. `?syncdebug` adds `window.__ishaiSync()` with the positions, and `window.__ishaiJoinMs` shows the last replay's time.

## Speech bubbles
The bubble's CSS is on `#say` only: the director's transcript uses the class `say` for spoken lines (`newLine('say')`), so a `.say` rule would float those lines over the page.
`placeSay()` puts the speaker's line over their head (`HEADS`), or at the edge for phone, radio and TV. It grows wide before tall (`max-width: min(94%, 560px)`) and is outlined, with its tail, in the speaker's own colour (`--who`); the line starts with their name in a tab of that colour (`#sayWho`). Colours: `INK` per character (Ishai teal, Noa pink, Mrs. Levi purple, Avi orange, Dana mustard, Tomer blue...), anyone new gets one from `INK_MORE` by name, always the same. `speaker()` gives `ink` (the colour itself) and `color` (a lighter tint for the dark chat and captions). New cast members should get an `INK` entry that no one else uses.

## Sitting
Wherever the scene draws Ishai in a seat (café and bar tables, the lecture room, the bus, the park bench), he faces front: the pose gets `seated:true`, and `fid()` never returns the look back (`turn`), a foot shift or a stretch for a seated pose, for him or anyone else. `SEATED_AT` keeps the errands (which stand him up) away from those seats.

## The hosts' booth
The last 10 host lines (`hostLine()`). It scrolls: a viewer can scroll up inside it, and it follows new lines only while they are at the bottom (`boothFollow`). While the studio recap plays (`studioOn`, `showOn`) the booth goes on in text only, with no voices or blips.

## Park animals
The far-lawn walkers (`crowdWalk` at y 34-35) pass behind the pond, never across the water. Pigeons peck on the path; when Ishai or a jogger comes close they fly off away from them (`PIGEONS`, per 40 s slot), and the flock lands again at the next slot. The strolling cat walks to the pond's edge, sits, and walks back; it never crosses the water.

## Hosts fill the silence
No AI, no writes, in the page. `hostFill()` runs on a 45 s clock slot (`FILL_SLOT`) after 10 s of quiet and never when a line is due (`talkSoon()`). `hostFillLine()` picks one: what he is passing while moving (`FILL[place]`), the place, the action (`FILL_DO`), facts worked out from the moments and the weather (`fillFacts()`), a sentence of today's or yesterday's summary (`fillRecap()`), or a cast member's memory (`fillPeople()`); one line in four at night. `fillPick()` walks a whole list before repeating. New places need a `FILL` entry and an `OVERHEARD` entry where crowds pass.

## The ambient layer
No AI, no writes, in the page, all from clock slots (`/api/ambient` serves both tables once at load; a deploy reloads pages).
- City timetable: `CITY` in the worker, weekly events by day, time and place (garbage truck, gas, alte-zachen cart, Friday flowers, market closing, school out, piano, kiosk queue, Mrs. Levi's game show). Where he is, the page plays its sound and sometimes a caption (`cityTick()`, 90 s slot), and the hosts mention it. `cityNote()` tells the story writer. Nothing runs during Shabbat.
- Building chat: `chatTick()`, a small card from the pack's `chat` times, otherwise a stock line every 40 minutes (`CHAT_STOCK`), 08:00-22:00, not on Shabbat.
- Texture pack: `texture` in `story/plan.json`, written daily by the planner (format in `story/season.md`), used only on its `date`. Adds to the hosts' fill, the DJ (`djLine()`), the regulars (`MUTTER`) and overheard lines through `pack()`. Without it the stock lines run alone.

## Pop out (picture-in-picture)
`⧉ POP OUT` copies the scene into a hidden video (`POP`, `popDraw()`), with the last lines drawn in as captions because the HTML doesn't reach the small window. While the page is hidden a worker's timer calls `frame()`, and the page's sound runs through the video (`Sound.popAudio()`). Works from the button on Android Chrome; automatic opening on leaving didn't.

## Screen awake
The eye button next to the bell keeps the phone's screen on while the page shows (`navigator.wakeLock`). On by default, saved on the device.

## Key-moment alerts
Web push for any viewer who taps the bell (`/sw.js`, `/api/push`, one KV key `push:<hash>` per device). `maybeAlert()` in `tick()` picks key moments (`alertText()`): a new item, a new person, a first appearance, a plot conversation. At most `ALERTS_A_DAY` (4) a day, `ALERT_GAP_MIN` (90) apart. The bell opens settings for each device: on or off, hours (default 08:00-23:00) and a daily limit, kept in its KV entry and checked in `deviceWants()`. Pushes carry no data; the device reads `/api/alert`. `sendAlerts()` sends `PUSH_BATCH` (30) per run, because of the free plan's request limit. The private key is the `VAPID_JWK` secret. The director page has a test button.

## Viewers' polls
Once an hour viewers vote on something small that someone else does to or for Ishai (worker: `pollTick()` in `tick()`, page: `pollTick()`, the card `#pollC` under the picture).
- Timing: written at the first tick of each hour (before hh:17) and opens at hh:20 Israel time for 60 s (`POLL_AT_MIN`, `POLL_SEC`), 08:20-20:20 (`POLL_FROM_H`, `POLL_TO_H`), not on Shabbat, not while he sleeps, never without the database. The card shows 20 minutes ahead with a countdown and the result stays 10 minutes (`POLL_SHOW_MIN`). All times come from the server, so everyone sees it open and close together.
- Sign-in: when the `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` secret is set, only people signed in with Google vote, once per account (setup: `docs/GOOGLE_SIGNIN.md`). The page loads Google's button only when the card needs it (`gisLoad()`, `authBox()`), posts the token to `/api/signin`, and the worker checks it against Google's public keys (`googleCheck()`: RS256 signature, issuer, audience = the client ID, expiry), then sets its own signed cookie `ishai_voter` (`g-<hash of the account number>.<expiry>.<HMAC from ADMIN_KEY>`, 180 days, `voterOf()`). No name or email is stored; the first name is shown back only in that browser. `/api/me`, `/api/signout`, `/privacy`. Signed-in votes skip the per-connection limit. The header's 👤 button (`#acct`, `acctShow()`, dialog `#acctF`, `acctFill()`) signs in or up at any time with the same Google button (`gisButton()`), shows who is signed in (amber outline), signs out, and links the director page for directors; it shows once `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` is set, and to the director before that (with the setup hint). On narrow phones the header icons wrap under the name as one row.
- Votes without sign-in (no `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`): anonymous, one per browser (a random id in `localStorage` `ishaiVoter`), at most `POLL_IP_MAX` (5) per connection per poll, checked against the poll's times on the server (`/api/vote`, `pollVote()`). Stored in the D1 database `ishai-polls` (binding `DB` in `wrangler.toml`; tables `votes (poll, voter, ip, opt, at)` with primary key (poll, voter) and `results`). KV is not used for votes: 1,000 writes a day and no safe counting.
- Result: counted once after closing (`pollResult()`), kept in `results` and read by everyone through `/api/poll?id=`. Percentages; the winner is highlighted. A tie is settled by `pollHash(poll id)`, the same for everyone. No votes: nothing changes. A host line announces it in the next moment.
- Writing: one small AI call (`writePoll()`, story writer) per poll; `pollCheck()` keeps only options that can really happen, otherwise a stock poll from `POLL_BANK`. Each option is `{label, who, kind, where, goal, forceText}`. Rules: someone from the cast acts, never Ishai (`POLL_NOT` also keeps Dr. Sasson and Yael out); kinds `serve`/`meet` (at one of that person's `hangouts`), `visit` (corridor), `message` (the only kind for `phoneOnly` people such as Gadi), `none` (at most one); no banned words (`POLL_BANNED`: sad, dangerous, romantic, life-changing, new things, any mention of votes or cameras); and a free time must exist (`pollStart()`).
- Effect only an hour or more later: the winner becomes a goal `poll-<yyyymmddhh>` (`g.poll`) starting at the first 15-minute mark at least `POLL_DELAY_MIN` (60) minutes after the vote where, for `POLL_LEN_MIN` (90) minutes, he is awake (08:00-22:00), it is not Shabbat, the place is open (`placeOpen()`, the same hours as `validate()`), and no other goal is planned or running (`pollFree()`), within `POLL_LOOK_H` (6) hours. Nobody in the story knows about the vote. Forced at its deadline like any goal.
- Director page: the coming poll with each option's goal, the last results, "Use a stock poll", "Skip this poll", "Turn polls off" (`pollBank`, `pollSkip`, `pollsOff`). `/api/health` → `polls`. Sandbox: "Viewers' poll" and "Poll result" (pretend votes, nothing sent).

## Trailer intro
`/intro` (`source/public/intro.html`, public, no AI, no writes) is a 93-second animated trailer: what the show is, the AI actors (with short in-character interviews), the hosts in the studio, the hourly vote (the cast only, never Ishai), and a montage that ends on a glimpse of poker night at Boaz's and of the director's band Penguin can Fly on the Pump House stage (frames rendered in the sandbox with a sample poker night and the band's real look, logo and beat map). Its pictures are the show's real art, exported from the scene builder as PNG data URIs inside the file (`ASSETS`), so new looks or places need a re-export: places with passers-by are exported with `crowd: true` as runs of consecutive 100 ms builder frames and played at 10 frames a second, so people and cars move instead of blinking. The screen is kept on while it plays (`navigator.wakeLock`). Music and sound are synthesised in the page. Voices use the browser's speech (`speechSynthesis`): a narrator reads every title card, and each actor, Ishai and the hosts read their own lines (`VOX`: an English voice by gender plus pitch and rate, `CUES` built from the cards and each shot's `say`); the music ducks while anyone speaks, and VOICES ON/OFF turns them off. A first-time visitor to `/` with no query string is sent there once (`ishaiIntroSeen` in `localStorage`, set as soon as the trailer page opens); the header's ▶ button (`#trailer`) opens it any time.

## Clothes and the closet
No AI, in the page. `WEAR` is what Ishai has on (`everyday`, `pajamas`, `gym`, `shabbat`, `towel`; `WEAR_FIT`). He changes only at the closet in the apartment (`CLOSET_X`, next to the bathroom door), for `DRESS_MS` with a red CENSORED bar over his middle only (`censorBox()`: head and legs stay in view), with the closet door open; the HUD says GETTING DRESSED. Never in front of anyone: with company in the apartment (`companyHere()`) he takes the clothes out (`GRAB_MS`, back to the camera, TAKING CLOTHES), carries them to the bathroom door (`BATH_DOOR_X`) and changes inside, out of sight (`actor.dressBath`: grab → go → in; `closetArrive()`, `dressStep()`, `bathChange()`). At home he wants `homeWear()` (pajamas in bed and late at night alone, the white shirt on Shabbat eve, otherwise everyday); going out he wants `outWear()` (sportswear for the gym, never pajamas or the towel). A new action at home (`setState()`), leaving (`startLeg()`) or coming home or out of the bathroom (`finishSwitch()`) sends him to the closet first when the two differ (`dressFirst()`, `doneDressing()`). Out of the shower he wears a towel round his waist until he changes. Outside he keeps what he left in, and rain or cold adds a jacket on top. The gym has a changing room (door with a hanger sign at the right edge, `GYM_LOCKER_X`): arriving in other clothes he walks in with his gym bag and changes there out of sight (`gymChange()`, from `finishSwitch()`), and before leaving in sportswear he changes back into `outWear()` of where he is going (`startLeg()`). It reuses the bathroom change (`actor.dressDoor`); the HUD says TO THE CHANGING ROOM. Joining resets `WEAR` from the moment before (`setState(..., true)`), so every viewer sees the same change.

## Events page: bands, concerts, promotions (v165)

## Scene builder
`/builder` (`source/public/builder.html`, dev only, linked from the director page) composes square pictures from the show's own art, with no AI and no writes. A hidden `/?builder` page (`startBuilder()`, `window.__builder`) draws frozen pictures with the real code: `scene()` for a place, `sprite()` for a character or object, and `PROPS` lists the objects (add new ones there; `menus()` offers them; `crop()` cuts a sign out of a scene, so its art stays in one place). The stage is 108 x 108 logical pixels with whole zoom only, and `paint(ctx, k)` draws the same picture at any whole size, so 5076 is ×47. Backgrounds are frozen: place, time, weather, frame, passers-by. Text layers use a 5 x 7 pixel font kept in `builder.html` (Latin letters only), wrap to the stage by zoom, and offer slogans from `menus().slogans`. Projects are saved in the browser (`localStorage`) or as a JSON file. Phase 2 plan: `docs/BUILDER_PHASE2.md`.

## Developer access

## Health check and quick fixes
- `GET /api/health` (public): recent moments, retry rate, recent rule problems, stuck counter, current scene, last AI error, art requests.
- Quick fixes without touching code: add to `story/plan.json` a `"fixes": [{"id": "fix-<date>-<hhmm>", "endScene": true, "note": "...", "minutes": 60}]` and bump `"version"`. `endScene` wraps up the running scene with a goodbye; `note` is a director note the story follows for `minutes`. Each fix id runs once.
- The health check can also apply a fix instantly without git: `GET /api/fix?token=<token>&endScene=1&note=...&minutes=45` (or `unstick=1`). The token is only in the scheduled task's prompt; the code holds its SHA-256 (`FIX_HASH`).
- Sleep moments may resemble each other (no repeat check while he sleeps); at night the prompt says it is dark and keeps him in bed.
- Preview of the planned day: `/?preview` plays `story/plan.json` beats in order (each beat's `preview.moments` with sample dialogue and host lines, or its force fields). `/api/plan` serves the plan. `&from=N` starts at moment N.
- Hourly (every rounded hour, 07:00+, not while he sleeps) one AI call writes: a text summary of today (`s.daySum`, shown in the SUMMARY tab with yesterday's `s.yesterdaySum`), the Studio recap show (`s.show`: sponsor intro, short yesterday, today focusing on the last hour, sponsor outro; the later in the day, the shorter yesterday) played on demand by the 📺 STUDIO button with Ishai live on the studio screen, and the short auto PiP recap (`s.studio`).
- Callers: every 30 minutes (08:00+, awake, no guest) `callIn()` makes one AI call: a random caller (name, town, mood: funny/angry/confused/superfan/conspiracy/grandma...) talks about the show, a random thing fetched from a free no-key API (joke, useless fact, random Wikipedia page, quote, advice, cat fact), or both; a host introduces them and answers. Stored as `s.call`; the page plays it when quiet in the 📞 ON THE LINE window (pixel phone), the hosts' column and full-screen captions.
