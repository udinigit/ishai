# Watching Ishai — guide for Claude

Live AI-written pixel-art reality feed on a free Cloudflare Worker. Pushing to `main` deploys automatically.
Owner (the director): udinigit. Read `docs/SCENE_GUIDELINES.md` before adding or changing any scenery, characters, props or animations.

## Repository
- `source/public/index.html` — the page (all drawing, animation, sound). `source/public/director.html` — director page. `source/public/sandbox.html` — test stage.
- `source/src/worker.template.js` — story engine (AI prompt, validation, director goals, plan merge, real-world data).
- `story/season.md`, `story/plan.json` — season arcs and the day's beats (written by the daily planner).
- `story/locals.json` — the nameless locals for small talk (see "Locals"), built into the worker and the page.
- `story/world.json` — the world map: every place, its kind (`story`, `pass` for page-only blocks, `vehicle`), its doors (`links`), and for story places the description and actions. `source/world.mjs` checks it and the build writes it into the page (`MAP`, `LOCATIONS`) and the worker (`NEXT`, `PLACES`, `LOCATIONS`, `WALK`). Edit the map there, never in the page or worker (their markers `/*__WORLD_MAP__*/{}`, `__WORLD_NEXT__` etc. are filled at build). Plan: `docs/WORLD_PLAN.md`.
- `source/public/icons/` — favicons and `og.png` (link preview for WhatsApp etc.), embedded by the build and served by the worker. Favicon = the studio eye; preview = Ishai under the eye.
- `src/vendor/` — third-party code the worker imports.
- `source/public/voices.js` — the shared voices (device voices, HD voices, the director's voice settings), inlined by the build into `index.html`, `intro.html` and `director.html` at their `/*__VOICES_JS__*/` marker (the build fails without it).
- `src/index.js` — generated. Build with `cd source && node build.mjs`. Never edit `src/index.js` by hand.
- Bump `VERSION` and commit with a clear message; Cloudflare deploys in ~30 s and open pages auto-reload.

## Non-negotiables
- Reality show of a real life ("The Truman Show"), not a sitcom: realistic people, physics and logistics. Ishai never knows he is filmed.
- No teleportation: every place is on the world map (see the guidelines) with real exits, entries and ways to get there.
- Follow `docs/SCENE_GUIDELINES.md` for anything visual.
- Fictional brands only (Cooola, HummusPrime, Wee Laddie Virgin Scotch, A Butcher Is Born, Tahini Tower Savings Bank, Yalla Yoga, FluffNap, Herzl Hardware, Kfar Kofi, Radio Herzl 88.2, ateBite (food delivery), Everything Else (store), Shuk Shalom (market), NESflicks (streaming), 8bitTV (Channel 8), Boolify (music station), RGBeat (Channel 6, music clips), PicSell (pictures, posters, printed shirts), …). Never real brands or their look-alike trade dress.
- Narration (`text`) is dev-only; viewers see the picture, spoken lines and the hosts (Zohar, Oren).
- Keep the free tiers in mind: Workers AI ~10k neurons/day, KV 1k writes/day.
- Half-moments: a moment may carry `then` ({action, text}): ~3 minutes in (`THEN_MS`), the page switches him to that action in the same place with the same people (`thenTick()` in `release()`, by the clock; skipped when he isn't settled there). `thenOf()` in `validate()` keeps it only for actions allowed in that place (never sleep, bathroom, radio/TV, walking). The next moment's prompt sees it; the hosts are told about it. No extra AI call or KV write.
- Coming up: `comingUp()` lists the next planned beats, lectures, Pump House and poker nights (not walks, visits or polls) with a viewer-facing hook (`teaser` on a plan beat, else built from who/where). The hosts' call gets it at most every 40 minutes (`s.lastTease`, `TEASE_RE`) and teases one in one line; the studio recap and the PiP end with a teaser. `/api/health` → `comingUp`.
- Conversation length (`TALK` in the worker): every moment with someone carries its own 10-16 lines; story blocks are 3 moments (`BLOCK_N`). `talkLevel()` brakes to 8-14 lines (and lets thin written-ahead moments through) when Gemini is missing/paused/failed in the last 30 min or Cloudflare passed ~6,000 neurons today. `/api/health` → `talk`.

## How moments are written (AI budget)
Two writers when the `GEMINI_API_KEY` secret is set (Cloudflare dashboard → Workers → ishai → Settings → Variables and Secrets): **Google Gemini (free tier, the newest stable Flash-Lite, or `GEMINI_MODEL`) writes the story** (blocks, live moments) and **Cloudflare Workers AI writes the hosts** (`addHosts()`: one call for a whole block's comments, plus the studio recaps and callers). Each is the other's backup (`runModel(env, messages, maxTokens, role)`); a Gemini 429 pauses it until its quota resets (midnight Pacific). The story prompt then leaves the hosts out (`HOSTS_APART`). Without the key everything runs on Cloudflare in one reply, as before. `/api/health` → `writersSetup` and `usage.gemini`. A Gemini call that fails falls back silently, so `tick()` saves the failures and the pause in the state (`s.geminiErrs`, last 8, and `s.geminiOutUntil`): memory is per isolate and `/api/health` may be a different one. Health shows `writersSetup.geminiErrors` and `usage.gemini.failed` / `failedBy` (per HTTP status).

Workers AI output costs ~6.5x input, so the engine avoids AI calls where it can (`tick()` in the worker, in this order):
- Night (asleep, 22:00-06:00): `nightEvent()` picks a sleep moment from a list. No AI.
- Planned beats: a beat's `preview.moments` (or `script`) are PLAYED AS WRITTEN once the beat starts (`scriptEvent()`): Ishai first walks there one place at a time (`travelEvent()`, no AI), then each moment plays in turn; the live AI then continues the scene and winds it down. The daily planner writes these moments as the finished scene.
- Live moments (one AI call each, spontaneous): radio/TV on (`liveCue()` + `onAirFor()`: a real headline, the real weather or the real station's genre is read by the DJ / Channel 9 and Ishai reacts; otherwise the AI writes the on-air line in `onAir`), and the moment the rain starts or stops. The on-air line is put first in `lines` with `who: "radio"` or `"tv"`; the page plays it (and keeps its own DJ quiet for 4 minutes). Between live radio/TV moments he just listens (`quietMedia()`, no AI).
- Everything else: blocks of up to 3 moments in one AI call (`askBlock()`, `s.queue`), shortened before the next planned beat. Each queued moment is validated when it plays; the block is dropped when anything else happens (a live moment, a beat starts, weather flips, a director note, stuck). A moment that breaks a rule in a new block is skipped and the next one takes over; a block with no usable moment falls back to one moment the old way.
- Places with music: the bar plays a real 24/7 rock station (Radio Paradise Rock Mix; backups from radio-browser in `s.world.barStations`), the café soft rock / pop at low volume (SomaFM Left Coast 70s, Radio Paradise Mellow Mix; backups `cafeStations`), the apartment radio as before. Every stream fades down quickly while anyone talks (`TALK_AT`, set by each typed character of speech) and comes back after; where the browser can't change the volume (iOS) it pauses and resumes instead.
- Rides: `bus` and `taxi` are real places. A story hop like street > market passes through one by itself on the page (`route()`, `rideKind()`), so the AI only uses them when something happens on board (one moment, at most two). `ride` on a public event tells the page which vehicle the text mentions. A friend riding with him follows him to the stop and gets on after him (`comp.boarded` in `compUpdate()`), then sits beside him on board (`rideMate()`). The taxi is shown from outside: a small yellow car, the size of the street's cars, driving through the looping city (`taxiSide()` over `cityDrive()`), with the driver, Ishai and a friend as heads in the windows.
- Planned moments may carry `delivery`, `message` and `update`, like the AI's. `update` ({"memory": "...", "mood": -1|0|1}, on the last moment with that person) is the only way a planned scene changes someone's memory and relationship.
- The story prompt carries the show's longer memory (`storySoFar()`: `s.yesterdaySum`, `s.daySum`, `PLAN.arcs`, clipped); the hosts' call gets a short `s.daySum`.
- What only the narration says: the story prompt tells the writer that anything in `text` the picture can't show (something brought or handed over, wet clothes, a smell, a sound, a message) must be named in `lines`, and the hosts' prompt tells them to name it when nobody does. `unseenHosts()` in `tick()` is the no-AI net: in a moment with talking, if the narration has someone holding, carrying, bringing or handing over something (`UNSEEN_CUE`), or soaked, and no line mentions it, a host adds one ("Dana's got a damp coat and a small terracotta pot..., in case you missed it.").
- Silent moments (no lines, no hosts: travel, waiting, quiet radio/TV, anything the writers left bare) get host lines in `tick()` with no AI (`quietHosts()`, reading out `text`). Viewers can't read `text`, so bare moments must never reach them. Inside a moment, the page fills the rest of the silence: see "Hosts fill the silence".
- A beat with `sceneAt` has a fixed start (the Thursday 17:00 improv class): Ishai travels from `startAt`, then waits with no AI until the story clock reaches `sceneAt`. The story clock runs 5 minutes ahead of the cron tick, and ticks land on the 6-minute marks, so `sceneAt` 16:58 plays at 16:59. The class uses the bar-only action `improv`, which only planned moments can set.
- History course (Strange But True, `story/course.json`: 96 topics in 7 strands that take turns, and the first lecture, written by hand): Sundays, Tuesdays and Wednesdays at 11:00, skipping holidays (`nextLectureAt()`). `courseTick()` runs in `tick()`: up to 30 hours ahead one AI call writes the lecture from the Wikipedia article (`generateLecture()`, about 13,000 to 16,000 tokens). Under 1,400 words it adds a second topic of the same strand; over 7,000 it splits into Part 1 and Part 2. `lectureMoments()` adds the break in the courtyard, the announcement of the next lecture and the walk out, and `scheduleLecture()` turns it into a goal with `course: true` (9 moments, about 48 minutes). The lecture goes ahead of any beat that has not begun. Missed: it moves to the next slot. Lines may come from `student1`-`student8` (nameless, `action: lecture` only). `publicState.course` feeds the blackboard, the courtyard sign and the SUMMARY tab. After a lecture its `note` goes into `s.course.memory` (last 10); `courseNote()` offers one to the AI at most once a day, only with company, each at most twice. The director page has the controls; `/api/lecture` (dev only) gives the sandbox the coming lecture. The hosts stay quiet in class: a moment with action `lecture` gets no host lines (cleared in `tick()`, and the lecture writer is told to leave `commentary` empty), the page's silence fill and callers skip it, and a vote result or survey line due then airs after the lecture (`s.surveySay`). The break in the courtyard and the walk out still have hosts.
- A beat with `sceneAt`: if he is with someone at the start, he excuses himself and goes.
- People: new `SEED_CAST` entries join the running show automatically. `phoneOnly` people (Gadi) are never drawn; a `message` in the AI's reply becomes a first line with `via` (text or email). Regulars are `SEED_CAST` entries with `regular`, matched by `REG` and `MUTTER` in the page.
- Shopping: Shuk Shalom market (food, CAM-10) and the Everything Else store next to it (everything except food, CAM-11) are a bus/taxi ride from the Herzl Street stop (`MAP.street.market` is a ride; the market has its own stop). Opening hours are enforced in `validate()`.
- ateBite food delivery: ordering is a moment on his phone/laptop; when the courier arrives the moment has `"delivery": true` (set by the AI, or detected from the text) and the page shows the orange-helmeted courier ringing, stepping in with the bag and leaving (`courierUpdate()`). Couriers also ride past on the roads now and then. Preview: `?loc=apartment&pose=idle&delivery=12`.
- The gym TV is live like the home TV: when Ishai is on the treadmill (`gym/exercising`) Channel 9 news/weather lines are written live and he reacts.
- AI replies are read by `parseAI()`: strict JSON, then `jsonrepair` (copied into `src/vendor/jsonrepair`; there is no npm step). A cut-off reply is cut at its last `}`, so its unfinished item is lost and no half sentence airs. `validate()` also splits an action like "walking with noa".
- `/api/health` shows `usage` (today's AI calls, tokens, estimated neurons, moments, no-AI moments, wasted block moments, `drops` by reason, `jsonFixed`, `jsonBad`, `blockSaved`), `block`, `lastBlockDrop` and `writers` (who wrote the last 10 moments).

## Drama: secrets and hooks (v212)
The season plan is in `story/season.md` ("Season 1, Part 2"); the daily planner writes these into `story/plan.json`, no extra AI calls.
- An arc may carry `secret`, `knows` (names), `revealOn` (YYYY-MM-DD) and `told`. Until `revealOn`, `secretsNow()` passes it to the story writer through `storySoFar()` ("Ishai does not know; the people named keep it hidden"), and to the hosts' call at most every `HINT_GAP_MIN` (45) minutes (`s.lastHint`): a hint, or said plainly to the viewers when `told`.
- `hook` {date, text}: today's open question, in the story prompt for that date only (`planFor()`). `previously` {date, text}: before 12:00 the studio recap opens with "Previously on Watching Ishai..." (also the no-AI `basicRecap()`).
- `/api/health` → `drama` (how many secrets are live, whether today has a hook and a previously; never their text).

## Everyone sees the same thing (sync)
- The page never reads the device clock for timing: `NOW()` is the server's time (`/api/time`, `syncClock()`). Use `NOW()`, not `Date.now()`, and pass it to `new Date(...)`.
- `schedule()` works out each moment's start and each line's slot from the moments alone (`TL`, `talkPlan()`, `lineMs()`): a line's slot fits its typing and its voice, and a long talk pushes the next moment back. Playback waits for those times (`untilT()`) and never for a device's speech. A viewer who arrives late joins by the clock, mid-line, and gets a voice only from the next line.
- Studio recaps, the auto Studio break and callers start at times worked out from the moments, not from what one browser sees (`studioStart()`, `showStart()`, `callStart()`, `slotAfter()`). The picture-in-picture recap plays on even UTC hours, the full break on odd ones; short no-AI PiP segments fill the gaps (see "Studio PiP between recaps"). Don't add `Math.random()` or per-browser checks to anything timed; the 📺 STUDIO button is the one personal exception.
- Movement runs in fixed 33 ms steps on that clock (`advance()`, `STEP`): anything that moves must take its time from `NOW()`, never `performance.now()` or `setTimeout`, because `NOW()` is the simulated time while steps run (`SIM`). A refresh or a returning tab replays the current moment fast and unseen (`joinMoment()`), starting 12 s before it. `update()` may not touch sound or anything a replay would repeat. Companions and the dog move during `render()`, so the replay renders every third step.
- Sound effects are picked from the 25 s clock slot, not at random.
- Voices: `Sound.say()` tries the speaker's own voice and falls back once to the device's default if it fails or hasn't started in 2.5 s. If the browser blocks speech, the button says TAP FOR VOICES. The sandbox status line shows `Sound.voiceInfo()`. Voices come from the shared `Voices` module (`source/public/voices.js`): device voices are ranked (`vScore()`: Edge Natural/Online, Google, Premium/Enhanced first; Apple novelty and eSpeak voices never) and matched to each character's gender (`GENDER`, else `genderOf()` from the cast card's look; `vGender()` from the voice's name), one voice per character from the best tier (`pickVoice()`), with gentle pitch and rate (`tone()` from `BASE`, the same numbers as the page's `VOICE` table).
- HD voices (the sound button's fourth mode, `SOUND: HD VOICES`; computers only: phones and tablets, iPads included, never get it, `Voices.phone` / `Voices.hdAllowed()`: the button cycles OFF/BLIPS/VOICES there, a saved `hd` becomes `voices`, the trailer never loads it, and the director page hides "Load HD voices here" and ▶HD): the Kokoro-82M neural model (`kokoro-js` from jsdelivr, weights from Hugging Face, downloaded once and cached by the browser) runs in a web worker built from `WORKER` in voices.js: on the graphics card (WebGPU, fp32, ~310 MB) when the browser has one, else on the processor (WASM, q8, ~90 MB); a warm-up line checks the card's output (silence or NaN drops to the processor) and measures the speed (`HD.rtf`, seconds of work per second of speech, shown as e.g. "0.05× speech time" in `Sound.voiceInfo()` and the director's Voices box). It starts only after the viewer has stayed on HD for 2.5 s; the button shows the download %. Each character has a fixed Kokoro voice (`KVOICE`, else a male/female pool from `K_LIST`). Every line the page knows (moments arrive about 5 minutes before they play; up to 12 minutes ahead), the PiP recap and the caller at their own start times, and the Studio break (`SHOW`, last in line, never dropped) are made ahead in speaking order (`Sound.prefetch()`, a 1 s interval over `TL`), cached (200 lines) and played through Web Audio (`hdPlay()`, so the pop-out hears them too); a line not ready in time is read by the device voice. Three failed lines in a row before any succeeds, or a failed download, show `SOUND: HD (FAILED)` and uses device voices; tapping back to HD retries. Once ready, `localStorage ishaiHD` = 1 tells the trailer this browser has the model. No server cost.
- Voice settings (director page → Voices): per character (the hosts, Ishai, the radio and TV, the DJs, the trailer narrator and the whole cast) gender, HD voice, pitch and speed; empty = automatic (the placeholders show what automatic gives). ▶ previews with this device's voice, ▶HD with the HD voice after "Load HD voices here". Saved by `voicesSave` (`cleanVoices()`) as `s.voiceCfg`, sent to the page in `publicState.voices` (`Voices.setConfig()`) and to the trailer by `/api/voices`.
- Can't match exactly: voices finishing, the room-sound loops, real radio streams. `?syncdebug` adds `window.__ishaiSync()` with the positions, and `window.__ishaiJoinMs` shows the last replay's time.

## Home life

The real station plays (`ambienceKey()` 'radio'), the notes float from the radio, and the DJ talks only when he is alone; the HUD keeps showing what he is doing. The story prompt tells the writer the radio is often on so he can hum along or answer the DJ.
- Page: when he is home alone with action `idle` (07:00-23:00), `homeNow()` gives most 150 s blocks (`HOME_BLOCK`) one longer activity from `HOME`: sweeping, the counter, washing up, the keyboard at the desk, the phone in the armchair or on the bed, folding laundry, a snack, a dance, a workout, coffee by the window, watering Moshe; on Shabbat only the ones without work or phone, plus resting and reading. Picked from the clock like the errands, so every viewer sees the same; the moment's own text picks it when it names one (`HOME_HINT`). Steps: `{to}` walk, `{pose, dur, to?, dy?}` stand (moving slowly with `to`; `dy` lifts him nearer the wall, eased in and out, so at the counter he stands at it instead of behind the island), `{seat, sp, dur}` drawn by the scene in that seat (`S()` / `SP()` in the apartment). New poses: sweep, wipe, fold, dance, keys, scroll, lounge. The corner label, the sound (`HOME_SFX`) and the hosts (`FILL_HOME`) follow it. His idle spot is by the kitchen counter (144), not the bed. Sandbox: the Home activity menu (`home(kind, skip)`).
- Worker: `homeLifeTick()` in `tick()` adds director goals with no AI: Falafel's walks (morning, midday, evening, `WALKS`, when he is home and has not walked him for 3.5 hours) and drop-in visits (`VISITS`, up to 3 a day, 09:00-21:00, after 30 minutes alone at home, 150 minutes apart, never someone he saw in the last 4 hours; Levi, Noa, Tomer, Avi). `pollFree()` keeps both clear of planned beats. `/api/health` shows `homeLife`.

## Places: hours and outing rates
One table, `story/places.json` (embedded by the build as `PLACE_RULES`; edit it, bump `VERSION`, push). Per place: `open` hours by day type (`week` = Sun-Thu, `fri`, `sat`; [from, to), 26 = 02:00 the next night; a missing day type = closed; no `open` = never closes: the streets, the park, the plaza), `rate` (% chance per hour of a random outing, `[[fromH, toH, %]]` per day type, `byDay` for one weekday, e.g. the bar on Thursdays), `weather` multipliers (`rain`, `hot` ≥ 30°C, `cold` ≤ 12°C), `with` (% a friend comes along), `meet` (% he meets someone there) and `people` (who, weighted). `event` places (the university, the lecture room, the Pump House hall, apt2) are for planned events only.
- `placeOpen()` reads it for `validate()` (entering a closed or event-only place is sent back with `closedWhy()`; someone already inside may stay; planned moments are exempt), the polls (`pollStart()`) and the outings. `placesNote()` tells the story prompt what is closed now (with when it opens) and which places are event-only.
- `outingTick()` in `tick()` (no AI): one roll per story hour while he is home, awake (07-23), alone, home for `homeMin` (20) minutes, with nothing planned for 90 minutes, at most `maxPerDay` (7) a day, `gapMin` (60) apart. `outingRate()` = the hour's row × the weather (0 if the place is closed now or within the hour; Shabbat uses `sat`); one random number against the rates in turn picks a place or none (the rates add up, roughly doubled in v241 so he is out far more). Then `with` (friends only, not regulars or people at work: `OUTING_AT_WORK`) or else `meet` picks a person from `people` not seen in the last 4 hours (`pickPerson()`); alone, the goal names a local for small talk (see "Locals"). It becomes a goal `outing-MMDD-HH` (`g.outing`, `outingGoal()`) for 70 minutes. Not in the teasers or the TV guide.
- Going out is no longer left to the AI (before v241 it mostly ignored the goal and he stayed home): `outingStep()` in `tick()` takes him there in one no-AI moment ("outing: on the way", `OUTING_GO`; the page walks him through every place on the way, by bus where it must) as soon as he is free (not asleep, in the bathroom or on the phone, nobody else with him). With a friend, the story brings the friend over first; after 20 minutes without them he goes alone. The friend walks with him with a few lines from `OUT_TALK`. Once there (`g.arrivedAt`), the AI writes the visit with the goal as its direction; leaving finishes the goal.
- Wandering on (`chainTick()`, no AI): after the shortest stay there (`stay[0]`, else 20 minutes), `CHAIN_PCT` (55%) of the time he heads on to another open place within 16 minutes' walk or a bus ride (never back to the street or to the gym), up to `CHAIN_MAX` (2) times an outing, a friend still with him coming along; else the story takes him home when it likes. `/api/health` → `outings` (`n`, `log`, `lastRoll` with every place's rate this hour and why it didn't roll, `lastChain`), `outingErr`.

## Line 88 at the stops: everyone rides the bus (v241)
No AI, no writes, in the page, all from the clock. Every stop (street, market, campus, promenade: `STOP_AT`) gets a bus every `BUS_EVERY` (3 minutes, 06:00-21:00, not on Shabbat: `busesRun()`): before it comes, 0-3 people walk to the stop and wait right of the sign (`stopWaiters()`, `STOP_SPOT`), the bus pulls in (`stopBus()`, door at the sign, `BUS_ST`), 0-2 riders step off and walk away (`stopAlight()`) and the waiting ones get on (`stopLife()`); brakes and doors are heard (`busstop` / `busgo`).

At his stop the ones going there too step off after him and walk away into the new place (`STOPOFF`). The taxi takes nobody else. Sandbox: `api.ride()` shows the plan.

## Whereabouts: nobody is in two places
- Page: `seenWith(id)` reads the timeline for the last moment someone spent with Ishai (where, when they parted, whether they came there with him). `awayFrom(id, loc)`: someone who parted from him elsewhere can't be at their post (`POST`, `POST_X`) or regular spot (`regHere()`) until they could have walked there (`WALK`, see below, at least `AWAY_MS`, 8 min). Meanwhile a cover barista / kiosk hand stands in. Someone who walks in WITH him to where they work (Dana from the street into Café Nahat) comes through the door behind him and then walks to their post (`toPost` in `compUpdate()`, `ARRIVED`); a late joiner just finds them at it. Leaving with him, they come out from their post (`leavingWith`, `fromPost`).
- Worker: `lastWith()` finds the same from `s.events`. `validate()` sends back a moment with someone who parted from him at another place less than `awayMins()` ago (`AWAY_MIN` 8); the lenient retry drops them from the moment.
- Walking time: page and worker read the same `WALK` table (minutes between every two story places), built from `story/world.json`: entering a story place costs 4 minutes, walking through a pass-through block 1 (`walkMin` overrides), the bus or taxi nothing; never under 8. The page's `HOP_MS` is only a fallback for places the table doesn't list. `stayNote()` tells the story prompt who has just left him where.

## Stay length per place
`stay: [min, max]` minutes per place in `story/places.json` (gym 40-100, café 20-120, bar 45-240, park 15-120, market 20-75, store 15-60, shops 5-40, plaza 10-90; streets, corridor and home have none). `stayMins()` counts from his first moment of the current run there. `validate()` sends back leaving before the minimum (the lenient retry keeps him there when nobody new is with him) and staying past the maximum; `stayFree()` lets him go early for a goal elsewhere, closing time, rain at the park/plaza/market, a director note or a stuck story. ## Health check and quick fixes

Never use `allowNoHosts` for a planned-only privilege.
