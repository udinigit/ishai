# Watching Ishai — Season 1

The daily planner reads this file and `plan.json`, then plans the next 24 hours.
You (the director) can edit anything here; changes go live with the next deploy.

## Rules of the show
- Everyday life in Tel Aviv: warm, funny, never cruel. Real weather and the Jewish calendar are real.
- Ishai celebrates Shabbat at home in a warm, traditional-but-relaxed way.
- About a third of each day stays unplanned so the live AI can improvise.
- 3-6 key beats per day, each with a time window. New props, places or animals need art first ("art" field).

## Arcs
1. **Dana and the basil cups** — Dana, the deadpan barista, draws basil leaves on Ishai's cups. Slow, shy romance over 1-2 weeks. Stages: he notices → he asks → a real conversation → she visits Moshe → first date.
2. **Saving Moshe** — Moshe the basil is wilting. Ishai tries everything (too much water, talking to him); Dana knows plants. Ends with Moshe thriving.
3. **The big client** — a client wants the logo "bigger but smaller". Paid work that pays for the TV; ends with a win or a funny disaster.
4. **Getting a TV** — Sunday: Avi's cousin offers an old TV (director goal "tv").
5. **Mrs. Levi's old radio** — Monday: she gives him her late husband's radio (director goal "radio").
